Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Welcome to Tab Split & Merger. Your privacy is extremely important to us.

1. Information Collection and Use

Tab Split & Merger is a browser extension designed to help you manage your browser tabs more efficiently. We want to be clear about how the extension works:

  • No Personal Data Collection: This extension does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, browsing history, or any other identifiable data.
  • Local Processing: All operations (moving tabs by domain, moving the current tab to another window, or merging one or all windows) are performed locally within your browser. No data is sent to any external servers.

2. Permissions

To function correctly, the extension requires the following permissions:

  • tabs: Required to read a tab's URL and hostname, and which tab you are using, so the extension can move matching tabs, move the current tab to another window, and keep that tab focused. This stays in your browser and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • windows: Required to create new windows and move tabs between existing windows.
  • contextMenus: Required to add the mover/merger options to your right-click menu.

We only use these permissions to perform the specific tasks described in the extension's core functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not share any information with third parties.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support link provided in the Chrome Web Store listing.
